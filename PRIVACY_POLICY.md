MYTRIX Bot — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

MYTRIX ("Bot", "we", "our", or "us") is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, utility, automation, welcome, and other server-management features.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, MYTRIX may process:

- Discord User ID
- Discord Server/Guild ID
- Discord Channel ID
- Discord Role ID
- Discord message or interaction information required to execute commands
- Server configuration settings
- Moderation-related information when moderation features are used

MYTRIX does not intentionally collect passwords, Discord account tokens, payment card information, or other sensitive authentication credentials.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by MYTRIX may be used to:

- Provide and operate bot commands
- Manage server configurations
- Provide moderation and security features
- Send welcome or automated messages
- Maintain logs when logging features are enabled
- Prevent abuse and troubleshoot technical problems
- Improve the reliability and functionality of the Bot

3. Data Storage

Some server configuration or feature-related information may be stored in a database to allow MYTRIX to continue functioning correctly after the Bot restarts.

We only retain information that is reasonably necessary for the Bot's functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

MYTRIX operates through Discord's platform and may use third-party infrastructure or services for hosting, databases, APIs, or other technical functionality.

Information processed by those services is handled according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or intentionally provide collected information to third parties for advertising purposes.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

- Operate the Bot
- Protect the Bot and its users
- Comply with applicable law
- Investigate abuse or security issues

6. Data Deletion

Server owners or authorized administrators may request deletion of MYTRIX-related stored server data.

For data deletion requests, contact the MYTRIX support team through the official support server.

7. Children's Privacy

MYTRIX is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children. Users should use Discord and MYTRIX in accordance with Discord's applicable age requirements and policies.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by MYTRIX. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published through the official MYTRIX website, support server, or other official communication channels.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, please contact the MYTRIX support team through the official support server.
